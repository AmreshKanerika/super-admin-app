How offboarding works
  • Offboard expires the organization's subscriptions immediately and blocks sign-in for every user. No data is deleted.
  • An expired organization can be reactivated at any time — its users get access back with their data intact.
  • Delete permanently is a separate, manual step for expired organizations: it archives and then destroys the data, database, login system and web address. It cannot be undone and never happens automatically.
