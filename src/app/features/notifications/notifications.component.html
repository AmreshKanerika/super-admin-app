How reminders work
  • When automatic reminders are on, the organization's admin is emailed at each timing chosen in Settings (for example 7 days before expiry).
  • Expiring soon lists active subscriptions ending within 30 days. Select rows and use Send reminder to email them now.
  • Every reminder, automatic or manual, appears in History. A failed reminder can be resent once the email settings or recipient are fixed.
  • Addresses under Also notify are copied on every reminder.
